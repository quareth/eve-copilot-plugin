# Environment and rules

Use this reference whenever legality, security band, spatial effects, entry
restrictions, objective mechanics, or seasonal rules affect ship or fit choice.

## Verify volatile rules at recommendation time

Use current official CCP support, patch notes, or event pages for:

- recently rebalanced hulls, modules, structures, NPCs, or system effects;
- factional-warfare complexes, battlefields, Military Campaigns, and seasonal
  sites;
- pirate insurgency corruption/suppression effects;
- ESS, Skyhook, sovereignty, command-node, and Upwell objective behavior;
- Abyssal weather, entry composition, suspect rules, and timers;
- Pochven access, standings, NPC debuffs/immunities, and sites;
- event site eligibility, spawns, keys, timers, and rewards;
- known and declared exploits.

## High-security space

Distinguish legal wars, duels/limited engagements, suspect baiting, kill rights,
ganking/anti-ganking, structure warfare, and PvE interruption. Check:

- legal target state and Crimewatch consequences;
- CONCORD response is punishment, not prevention;
- weapons, aggression, session, and logoff timers;
- war eligibility, allies, War HQ, and structure objectives;
- gate/station docking and tether behavior;
- neutral assistance and remote support consequences;
- cargo/loot exposure and pod/implant policy.

Ordinary warp disruption bubbles are not available. This does not make travel
or a legal war target safe.

For suicide or anti-gank analysis, distinguish mechanical feasibility from
legality and policy. Do not help exploit CONCORD or game enforcement; consult
the current known-exploits page when behavior approaches an enforcement edge.

## Low-security space

Ordinary bubbles are not available, but tackle, smartbombs, gate/station guns,
security-status loss, suspect/criminal states, weapons timers, and capital
escalation matter. Determine whether aggression occurs on a gate/station,
inside a complex, at an objective, or in open space.

For factional warfare and insurgencies, verify:

- current enlistment and legal combat context;
- frontline, command, rearguard, corruption, or suppression state;
- exact complex or objective entry restrictions;
- site fleet-size, hull, faction, or seasonal constraints;
- capture/scoring conditions and NPC presence;
- battlefield or campaign effects.

Do not generalize one complex's restrictions to another.

## Null-security space

Assume bubbles, drag/stop bubbles, interdictors, heavy interdictors, bombs,
cynos, bridges, hotdrops, and capital escalation may be present. Evaluate:

- bubble escape and conditional nullification;
- cloak/propulsion travel behavior and decloak threats;
- sovereignty owner, cyno jammer/beacon, Ansiblex or bridge access;
- ESS, Skyhook, sovereignty, and structure local restrictions;
- filament availability and extraction;
- jump range, fatigue, fuel, and staging;
- hostile response time and escalation path.

Public quietness is not proof a route, objective, or ratting system is safe.

## Wormhole space

Class, system effect, static connections, hole mass, jump mass, remaining
life/mass, polarization, and chain control can dominate the fit. ESI does not
provide a current chain. Use authorized bookmarks/private maps and pilot
observations.

Require:

- probes and a recovery scanner;
- bookmarked entrances/exits and safe locations;
- mass budget for entry, reinforcements, rolling, and extraction;
- polarization tracking;
- effect-aware weapons, tank, capacitor, remote repair, and mass;
- cloak/D-scan discipline and a response to new signatures;
- loot and pod extraction policy.

Do not apply known-space activity statistics as proof of J-space activity or
safety.

## Pochven

Verify current entry and exit methods, standings requirements, system effects,
NPC factions and standings consequences, current debuffs/immunities, site rules,
and travel topology. Treat most plans as PvPvE: player fleets, NPC response,
limited extraction, and resupply must be solved together.

## Deadspace, acceleration gates, and site effects

For every named encounter, resolve:

- exact hull and fleet entry limits;
- MWD, MJD, cloak, warp, cyno, or refit restrictions;
- keys/tags/filaments and consumption;
- boundaries, clouds, weather, system effects, or random modifiers;
- objective damage, repair, link, capture, and timer behavior;
- whether warping out is possible and what is lost by doing so.

Eligibility is a hard gate. Do not recommend entry when the site name or
restriction remains ambiguous.

## Capital and structure space restrictions

Verify docking/tether access, cyno and jump restrictions, security limitations,
structure state, vulnerability window, damage cap, repair timer, siege/triage
commitment, and extraction before selecting a capital. Some capitals or modes
cannot be safely or legally used merely because the pilot can board them.

## Engagement timers and escape

Fit and operating advice should distinguish:

- weapons/aggression timer;
- suspect/criminal timer;
- limited engagement or war legality;
- session change and docking/tether restrictions;
- logoff timer;
- jump fatigue and polarization;
- bastion, siege, triage, cyno, link, entosis, or objective commitment.

An escape module is not an escape plan if a timer or objective state prevents
its use.

## Rule-confidence output

For every decisive restriction, report:

- the exact activity/environment it applies to;
- the current source and date checked;
- whether the rule was authoritative, observed, or inferred;
- any unresolved condition the pilot must verify in the client.

Avoid copying long rules into the answer. State only what changes the ship,
fit, entry decision, or operating plan.
