# PvE encounters

Use this reference for combat against NPCs. Resolve the exact encounter, tier,
wave, faction, trigger, objective, and environmental effect before fitting.
“PvE fit” is not a sufficient context.

## PvE success contract

Define which outcome matters:

- safe first completion;
- repeatable completion with a survival margin;
- fastest completion under a risk or budget limit;
- maximum income after ammunition, consumables, travel, and loss risk;
- solo completion versus an intended fleet role;
- escalation running, loot extraction, standings, LP, or objective progress;
- survival under likely player interruption.

Fit against required incoming damage, damage types, EWAR, neuts, webs, scrams,
target size/speed, spawn range, trigger order, room gates, travel, timer, and
escape restrictions. Distinguish a mechanically valid fit from a proven
encounter-capable fit.

## Encounter verification checklist

For the exact site or mission, establish:

- allowed hull class, fleet size, key, filament, standing, or access condition;
- NPC faction and damage dealt/weakness, including omni or changing profiles;
- initial and reinforcement ranges, wave triggers, objective targets, and
  completion condition;
- scrams, webs, neuts, EWAR, remote repair, target painters, defender behavior,
  anti-drone pressure, or capital escalation;
- incoming peak versus sustained damage and whether damage can be reduced by
  range, speed, signature, target order, or warping;
- travel/deadspace distance, acceleration gates, MWD/MJD/cloak restrictions,
  boundary, timer, weather, system effects, and extraction;
- cargo and endurance needs: ammunition, cap charges, paste, drones, probes,
  mobile depot, MTU, keys, filaments, boosters, and loot space;
- player-interruption risk in the selected space.

Use current CCP rules first for eligibility and volatile mechanics. Use
maintained encounter guides for wave details, then clearly label them by date
and verify dangerous triggers where possible.

## Missions, epic arcs, COSMOS, and level 5

Resolve the mission title and level; different pockets in the same mission can
change damage, EWAR, travel, triggers, and objectives. Fit for the exact NPC
faction and pocket, not only the agent faction.

- Early missions favor low execution burden and low replacement cost.
- Level 4 choices often trade travel/application against battleship or marauder
  damage and tank.
- Level 5 missions are commonly lowsec, neut-heavy, and interruption-prone;
  account for scouting, travel, disposable versus expensive hull policy, and
  player threats.
- Marauder/bastion fits must model immobility, cycle commitment, cap pressure,
  incoming player tackle, and exit timing—not only PvE tank.
- COSMOS and epic-arc steps may be one-time, standings-sensitive, or gated;
  verify the exact objective before risking a specialized ship.

State target priority, trigger discipline, ammunition, bastion/repair state,
and warp-out threshold.

## Anomic and Burner missions

Treat every Burner as a specialized encounter. Resolve whether it is agent,
team, or base content and verify exact hull limits, opponent propulsion,
scram/web/neut/EWAR, repair behavior, damage profile, ammunition, heat, and
piloting method. Do not substitute a generic assault-frigate or cruiser fit.

If exact current encounter mechanics are not verified, do not recommend entry
with an expensive specialized fit.

## Belt, anomaly, and officer ratting

Choose hull and fit from NPC class, spawn range, damage/application, chain or
escalation goal, system security, travel, salvage/loot, local intel, and player
interruption. For drone or AFK-style fits, explicitly account for NPC target
switching, anti-drone pressure, disconnects, and the pilot's real attention.

Officer hunting and rare spawns increase loot and PvP exposure. Include probes,
cargo/extraction, cyno risk, and a loss policy rather than optimizing only tick
rate.

## Combat signatures, DED complexes, unrated sites, and escalations

Resolve the exact site name and rating. Check acceleration-gate hull limits,
keys/tags, travel through hostile space, overseer or structure objective,
damage spikes, scrams/neuts/EWAR, room triggers, and loot extraction. Escalation
travel may make warp speed, probe capability, refit options, and covert hauling
more valuable than local paper damage.

Do not infer safety from a signature name. Some exploration-named sites contain
guarded or highly dangerous combat.

## Strongholds and diamond NPCs

Pirate Strongholds and diamond response fleets are dynamic fleet encounters,
not ordinary static anomalies. Verify current security-space availability,
response behavior, mining/hauler interactions, tackle/EWAR/logistics, escalation,
structure objective, and fleet composition. Do not recommend a solo entry from
a normal ratting-fit comparison.

## Besieged and hazardous exploration sites

Distinguish Besieged facilities from Ghost/Covert Research sites and from
ordinary data/relic content. Verify the exact site, NPC waves, damage, EWAR,
timers or explosions, access space, and objective. If identification remains
ambiguous, tell the pilot to hold or skip.

## Incursions

Treat the community or FC doctrine as mandatory. Establish site class, fleet
role, required tank/resists, logistics and cap chain, damage/application,
contest strategy, anchor, broadcasts, tags, and minimum performance thresholds.
Do not recommend an off-doctrine fit because it is individually valid.

Verify current influence and system/site context. Include ammunition, drones,
cap charges, paste, boosters, implants, and replacement requirements specified
by the group.

## Homefront Operations

Resolve the exact operation and intended fleet size. Homefronts are coordinated
objectives with role and mechanic checks; one ship may be unable to perform the
site even with sufficient tank. Allocate required repair, hauling, mining,
EWAR, damage, or objective interactions and verify current access rules.

## Abyssal Deadspace

Resolve tier, filament type/weather, chosen ship class, and fleet size. Verify
current allowed composition, entry safety, suspect consequences where relevant,
the hard completion timer, boundary behavior, weather modifiers, room spawn
families, tackle/neuts/EWAR, drone loss, and total-loss conditions.

Evaluate at least:

- weakest weather-modified resistance and incoming damage;
- application to both small fast and large targets;
- propulsion and boundary control;
- capacitor under neuts and repeated rooms;
- target priorities and dangerous spawn combinations;
- ammunition, drones, boosters, heat, and repair paste for three rooms;
- disconnect, trace/camp, and exit plan.

Abyss recommendations require an explicit conservative tier ceiling for the
exact pilot and fit. Do not infer tier capability by scaling a lower-tier fit.

## Wormhole Sleeper and Drifter PvE

Resolve wormhole class, system effect, anomaly/signature, site wave, escalation
conditions, and fleet composition. Sleepers may switch targets, pressure drones,
neut, web, scram, and remote repair. Capital escalations, Drifters, or special
sites require their exact mechanics.

Account for probes, chain security, hole mass/life, polarization, rolling or
crit control, loot hauling, mobile depot/refit, salvaging, and player ambush.
The fit is incomplete without a recovery and extraction plan.

## Pochven PvE

Resolve standings/access, site type, NPC factions, system effects, travel and
extraction, fleet roles, contest behavior, and current NPC debuffs/immunities.
Expect simultaneous player and NPC pressure. Fit the fleet for site completion
and PvP interruption rather than treating them as separate trips.

## Factional warfare, insurgency, and campaign PvE

First decide whether the objective is NPC completion, capture progress, player
combat, or campaign contribution. Verify exact current site restrictions and
scoring. A PvE-maximized ship may be unable to hold the site against a player;
state whether the fit chooses completion speed, contest capability, escape, or
a balanced PvPvE compromise.

## Capital PvE and CRAB beacons

Verify current beacon eligibility, system restrictions, capital hull behavior,
site waves, tackle, cap pressure, reinforcement risk, and reward mechanics.
Require cyno/support, escalation, rescue, refit, fuel, and extraction plans.
Never recommend committing a capital based only on local tank and damage.

## Seasonal event sites

Treat event availability, site rules, spawn tables, acceleration gates,
environmental effects, rewards, and balance as volatile. Check current official
event material at recommendation time. Label older community fits as historical
until revalidated.

## PvE output additions

Alongside the exact fit, report:

- the exact encounter and verified version/date;
- conservative operating ceiling or supported tier;
- expected damage/tank/application assumptions;
- target and trigger priority;
- module, ammunition, drone, heat, capacitor, and repair states;
- warp-out, abort, boundary, or timer thresholds;
- player-interruption and extraction plan;
- any decisive value that still needs in-game simulation or a current guide.
