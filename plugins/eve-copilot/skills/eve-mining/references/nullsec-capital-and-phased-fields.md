# Nullsec, capitals, and Phased Fields

## Nullsec operation design

Distinguish NPC nullsec from sovereign nullsec. Confirm current sovereignty
upgrades, anomaly/site eligibility, docking and tether access, blue-list and
intel policy, bubbles, cynos, jump logistics, local markets, hauling path, and
response fleet. Do not infer private intel channels or defense readiness from
sovereignty ownership.

Compare the operation scale:

- cheap/mobile expedition for untrusted or temporary access;
- defensive or supported barge fleet with scout, compression, and hauling;
- high-output exhumer fleet under survey allocation and response coverage;
- capital support only where site, access, fuel, defense, replacement, and
  extraction justify the commitment.

Mercoxit requires deep-core equipment and cloud procedure. Capital-grade ore
or ice sites still need exact current eligibility and NPC response. Account for
bubbles, covert hunters, cynos, interdictors, ESS/strategic traffic, and the
value accumulated on grid and in the hauling chain.

## Rorqual and capital commitment

A Rorqual is a fleet system, not a standalone yield upgrade. Before recommending
or coring one, require:

- current site and space eligibility;
- Capital Industrial Ships and all material core, compressor, burst, drone,
  tank, and support requirements;
- fuel, exact compressor and burst charges, storage, hauling, replacement
  drones, and consumables;
- cyno/jump/extraction plan under current mechanics;
- scouts, standing defense, escalation authority, and realistic response time;
- core-off and core-on operating states plus explicit stop/evacuation triggers;
- loss policy for hull, fittings, drones, implants, and trapped material.

PANIC or a strong tank buys response time under its current rules; it does not
create a response fleet or guarantee survival. If support is vague, recommend a
smaller command ship, subcapital fleet, or mobile expedition branch.

## Phased Fields and Prismaticite

Treat a Phased Field as a staged strategic fleet operation. Refresh current
official site mechanics before every plan because stage visibility, anchor
requirements, phase energy, public reveal, and rewards are volatile.

The operation generally needs:

1. scanner/prober and reliable bookmarks;
2. eligible miners for ordinary/high-grade resources and Prismaticite;
3. Mobile Phase Anchors plus replacements and current deployment knowledge;
4. enough eligible industrial-command phase energy for intended efficiency;
5. core, boost, compression, fuel, and hauling policy;
6. scouts and security for increasing exposure;
7. a deliberate choice to extract before or contest the richest/public stage;
8. disposition plan for Prismaticite: sale, random reprocessing, or controlled
   reaction with gas and structure access.

Do not assume an anchor is deployed, powered, owned, or safe. The MCP cannot see
its live state. Treat phase efficiency, remaining timer, and grid control as
pilot/fleet observations unless a current tool explicitly supplies them.

## Active triggers

Align or begin extraction when intel coverage lapses, new probes or hostile
ships appear, an entry pipe changes, response readiness drops, a core cycle
would exceed the safe window, hauling backs up, or the stage is about to become
more exposed. An FC or capital commander controls fleet commitment; present any
deviation for approval.
