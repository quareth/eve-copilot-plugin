# Ore, ice, gas, and moon resources

Resolve the exact resource through the active SDE and refresh current site
rules. Similar-looking resources can require different modules, crystals,
skills, hazards, compression, or disposition.

## Asteroid ore and crystals

Ordinary ore uses mining lasers, strip miners, or mining drones as allowed by
the hull. Modulated and ORE deep-core modules require a compatible crystal;
wrong-family crystals do not provide the intended resource benefit.

The persistent crystal families include simple, coherent, variegated, complex,
Mercoxit, abyssal, and erratic resources. Resolve the current family and
processing skill from SDE rather than reproducing a potentially stale table in
the recommendation.

Choose Type A or other low-residue operation when conserving a shared,
high-value, moon, or strategic rock matters. Use higher-residue choices only
when current mechanics and the fleet's explicit policy value clearing speed or
denial over recovered volume. Residue is destruction, not extra hold yield.

Short-cycle ore when the remaining volume is below a full cycle and survey
information is reliable. Factor travel between rocks, range, lock time, and
depletion into realized output.

## Mercoxit

Mercoxit is a separate deep-core branch:

- require Deep Core Mining, a compatible deep-core module, and Mercoxit
  crystal;
- account for the current local cloud hazard and use range/tank accordingly;
- verify the actual nullsec or Pochven opportunity rather than assuming any
  rare-ore site contains it;
- include crystal supply, compression, hostile exposure, and extraction.

## Ice

Ice uses eligible ice mining lasers, ice harvesters, or ice drones. Each module
cycle must complete to produce a block, so do not recommend ore-style
short-cycling. Confirm hold room before starting the next cycle.

Compare cycle reduction, critical yield mechanics, hold capacity, CPU and rig
pressure, mobility, and compression. An ice drone's travel time and loss risk
are distinct from harvester output. The active field, depletion, and respawn
state are live facts, not ESI facts.

## Gas

Separate three branches:

- **Mykoserocin:** booster/capital-chain gas with location-dependent sites;
- **Cytoserocin:** low/null constellation gas where specific clouds can produce
  hazardous damage;
- **Fullerite:** wormhole gas with scanning, delayed Sleeper response, no Local,
  chain control, and bulky hauling.

Use Gas Cloud Harvesters/Scoops appropriate to the hull and current rules.
Mining Laser Upgrades and resource crystals do not improve gas harvesting.
Specify probes or a scanner dependency, exact cloud tank where hazardous,
positioning, sleeper/NPC procedure, and extraction. Gas compression requires
compatible industrial-command support; never assume public access.

## Moon ore

Moon mining is an access-and-schedule operation as well as a fit decision.
Confirm the refinery pull, fracture window, access list, tax/ownership rule,
fleet residue policy, boosts, compression, hauling, and defense. Use authorized
corporation timers where available; otherwise treat timing and access as
pilot-reported.

Conservation often favors low-residue modules/crystals and coordinated survey
allocation. Public or contested pulls shift the choice toward cheaper,
defensive, or mobile hulls and a shorter extraction threshold.

## Mission, Homefront, campaign, and special resources

Mission ore, Homefront resources, event ores, campaign deposits, and newly
introduced resources can have restricted gates, payout use, fleet design,
nonstandard modules, or temporary availability. Before fitting:

1. resolve the exact site and item name;
2. verify current official entry, ship, fleet, and completion rules;
3. confirm whether material must be deposited, transported, reprocessed,
   reacted, or merely mined for credit;
4. avoid assuming ordinary belt economics or ownership;
5. fit for the site's actual NPC, timer, contest, and extraction conditions.

Prismaticite and Phased Fields are routed to the nullsec/Phased Field reference.
When its reaction path matters, compare controlled mineral output against
ordinary random reprocessing and include gas input plus structure access.
