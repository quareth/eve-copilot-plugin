# Fleet boosts, compression, and logistics

## Separate fleet roles

A complete operation may require:

- miners to extract and manage cycles/crystals;
- booster to apply the exact range, cycle, residue/critical, or preservation
  effects allowed by current charges and skills;
- compressor with the correct resource service;
- survey coordinator to allocate rocks and prevent cycle waste;
- hauler to move raw/compressed material, fuel, anchors, and consumables;
- scanner/scout for signatures, routes, wormholes, and warnings;
- security to clear NPCs, screen tackle, or buy extraction time;
- FC to own site, residue, core, contest, and evacuation policy;
- logistics/refining planner to choose compression, reprocessing, reactions,
  taxes, and sale/manufacturing destination.

Do not assume one command hull performs all roles. Compare each support pilot's
benefit against the mining output and attention that pilot gives up.

## Select support by scale and commitment

- **Outrider:** mobile bursts and repositioning/MJFG utility without
  compression; useful when mobility is more valuable than a core-supported
  service.
- **Porpoise:** economical small-fleet boosts plus asteroid/gas compression;
  requires exact core, compressor, fuel, tank, and escape policy.
- **Orca:** broader compression, storage, ship bay, and subcapital support;
  valuable enough that exposure and core commitment must be justified.
- **Rorqual:** capital-grade support/drone operation only with site eligibility,
  fuel, scouts, defense, escalation authority, and extraction.
- **Odysseus:** expedition scanning, covert movement, and gas support; not an
  ore/ice compression substitute.

Refresh the exact current burst, compression, and hull bonuses. Specify burst
charges by intended effect; “mining boosts” is not an adequate fit description.

## Compression and core policy

Confirm that the service accepts the exact resource. Asteroid ore, gas, ice,
moon ore, and special resources do not automatically share the same compressor
or skill requirements.

Define:

- core-on conditions and who authorizes commitment;
- core-off warning state and the cycle window needed to extract;
- fuel stock and reserve;
- compressor activation and access policy;
- fleet range/positioning and movement limits;
- storage ownership and material handoff;
- evacuation behavior if compression becomes unavailable.

Because ESI cannot see live activation, boosts, fleet range, or service access,
model intended states and ask for one pilot observation in live guidance when
it changes the next action.

## Survey, cycles, and residue

Use current survey information to allocate rocks, reduce wasted cycle time, and
keep miners from overcommitting to the same remainder. State who broadcasts or
assigns targets. For ice, never plan partial-cycle yield. For ore/gas, use
short-cycling only when the current mechanics and reliable remaining-volume
estimate support it.

The fleet must explicitly choose whether it conserves shared rock, maximizes
time-normalized extraction, clears a site, or denies material. Select crystals,
modules, bursts, and allocation accordingly. Never silently override a moon or
corporation residue policy.

## Hauling and disposition

Plan the whole path:

```text
site -> hold/fleet storage -> compression -> hauler -> staging
     -> refining or reaction -> manufacturing input or sale
```

Compare miner hold time, hauler cadence, compression ratio, route exposure,
structure access, refining yield, reactions, taxes, current prices, and support
pilot opportunity cost. Add replacement crystals, probes, charges, fuel,
anchors, paste, and drones to the logistics manifest.

Define a threshold for dispatching the hauler and a fallback when the hauler,
compressor, structure, or route is unavailable. Valuable compressed cargo can
move the highest risk from the mining grid to the transport leg.

## Fleet handoff

State every dependency explicitly: booster hull, skills, charges, compressor,
core, fuel, survey source, hauler capacity/cadence, scouts, security, access,
and FC authority. If a dependency is not confirmed, provide a degraded plan
that remains usable without it.
