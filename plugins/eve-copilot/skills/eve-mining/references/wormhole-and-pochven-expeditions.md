# Wormhole and Pochven expeditions

## Wormhole mining

Treat J-space mining as an expedition with chain control, not a quiet belt.
There is no Local intelligence. Public known-space jump/kill rows do not measure
wormhole activity.

For ore or Fullerite gas:

1. scan and bookmark the entrance, site, safes, perches, and every required
   return connection;
2. record observed wormhole destination, mass, lifetime, polarization, and
   rolling status; never invent a connection from static topology;
3. select Venture/Prospect or a larger fleet only after comparing signature,
   mobility, cloak, probes, hold, hauling, site control, and replacement cost;
4. identify exact gas clouds and the current delayed Sleeper response; define
   whether the miners leave, a combat group clears, or the site is abandoned;
5. account for ore/gas volume, compression support, hole mass, hauler trips, and
   the effect of valuable command ships on the chain;
6. keep a credible alternate exit and extract before cargo value, session time,
   or hole degradation removes it.

Unsecured and Forgotten gas-site names imply guarded content until exact
current mechanics prove otherwise. Never recommend remaining for “one more
cycle” when combat probes, an unknown connection, mass/lifetime degradation, or
polarization removes the escape margin.

Industrial command ships and barges can be valid after site control, but their
mass, visibility, core commitment, and hauling dependency must be part of the
plan. An Odysseus or covert expedition package can support discovery and gas;
it does not create industrial-command compression.

## Pochven mining

Pochven combines delayed Local, dangerous roaming NPC/player fleets, special
travel and standings constraints, limited logistics, and valuable ore/ice/gas
opportunities under current rules. Verify the exact deposit, standings/access,
NPC factions, gates/filaments/wormholes, docking, compression, and exit method.

Build around:

- low-cost or mobile expedition hulls unless an organized fleet controls the
  site and logistics;
- continuous D-scan and scout coverage rather than Local silence;
- exact damage/tank and response for the deposit's roaming NPCs;
- Mercoxit deep-core requirements or gas/ice-specific modules where applicable;
- short extraction thresholds, compact/filament logistics, and a fallback exit;
- no assumed public compression, market, station access, or friendly response.

Use current official rules for any home-system, standings, filament, or site
restriction. A quiet public activity snapshot does not establish an empty
Pochven grid.

## Live response

In either environment, immediate danger advice begins with the action: stop
cycles, recall or abandon drones as time permits, align/warp/cloak according to
the real fit, or follow the fleet extraction call. Then request at most one
missing observation such as probes on D-scan, tackle status, hole condition, or
confirmed exit.
