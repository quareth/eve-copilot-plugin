# Highsec and lowsec

## Exordium and starter operations

Use the current official tutorial/career rules and site restrictions. Favor
low-cost eligible hulls, simple valid fits, clear hold/unload guidance, and the
skills the pilot can use now. Do not import low/null doctrines or assume a
temporary starter resource behaves like permanent belt ore.

## Highsec

Highsec offers belts, anomalies, ice systems, missions/Homefronts, public or
private moon pulls, and some gas opportunities under current distribution.
Its risks are different, not absent: suicide ganks, war targets, suspect bait,
competition, bumping, NPCs, and hauling exposure still change the fit.

Compare:

- independent hold mining when station trips dominate;
- supported yield mining when hauling/compression and rock allocation exist;
- defensive hulls and cheaper modules when exposure or replacement matters;
- ice-specialized operation only after confirming an active eligible field;
- moon fits under the owner's access and residue policy;
- mission/campaign fits under exact current gate and completion rules.

Use current nearby market and route evidence when disposition matters. Account
for refining structure, taxes, hauling, ore compression, and gank exposure.
Do not call an expensive tanked exhumer or Orca “AFK-safe.” CONCORD response is
punishment after aggression, not preventive protection.

Industrial-command support must justify its pilot opportunity cost and core
commitment. An Orca is a fleet/logistics platform; compare it against another
miner plus hauling rather than assuming it is the default solo upgrade.

## Lowsec

Lowsec adds stronger player exposure, limited gate safety, cynos, factional
warfare traffic, pirate activity, richer resource branches, gas constellations,
moon operations, and Phased Fields under current rules. Local exists, but it is
only one warning source.

Build the operation around:

- scouts and route alternatives around camps;
- cheap/mobile Venture, Prospect, Endurance, or newer specialist branches for
  day trips;
- defensive barges only when site control, response time, and extraction make
  their exposure credible;
- cloak/probes, safes, instadock/undock bookmarks, and pre-aligned exits where
  appropriate;
- exact Cytoserocin cloud damage and NPC response;
- short cargo/time extraction thresholds;
- no assumed bubbles, but full accounting for tackle, probes, cynos, and
  capitals permitted by current rules.

For organized moon or command-ship operations, specify scout coverage,
security response, core-on/core-off policy, compression, hauling, and who can
order evacuation. Do not commit a Rorqual or valuable support ship without a
credible defense and extraction plan.

## Known-space evidence

Use current ESI route and latest activity/kill evidence only as context. It does
not reveal current Local, gate camps, miners, belts, gas signatures, ice field
state, moon access, or hostile intent. State snapshot freshness and coverage;
missing rows are unknown, not zero.
