# Preparation profile

Use this profile before undocking or when the pilot asks for a ship, fit, route,
trip split, or evaluation of a specific courier contract.

## Build the move in five passes

1. **Objective:** establish cargo, origin, destination, deadline or session,
   return requirement, and success condition.
2. **Manifest:** establish cargo class, estimated volume and value, packaging,
   specialized-hold eligibility, collateral when applicable, and any item that
   cannot be repackaged or split.
3. **Ship:** start from owned ships and current skills. Select one practical
   hull, fit it for the actual space and cargo, validate the fit, and estimate
   trips from sourced or pilot-reported capacity.
4. **Route:** calculate the loaded route and the pickup leg when relevant.
   Compare a safer route only when it creates a useful choice; annotate current
   public risk evidence without pretending it is gate intel.
5. **Procedure:** define pickup, load split, manual travel, destination check,
   delivery, and simple hold, wait, dock, turn-back, or reroute triggers.

For a new pilot, prefer an affordable owned T1 or specialized hauler when it
fits the cargo and route. Favor attentive manual travel, moderate loads, useful
tank, and fewer unfamiliar mechanics over maximum theoretical capacity. Do not
recommend a T2 transport, freighter, or capital merely because it is the final
progression step.

## Ask minimally

Retrieve MCP facts first, then ask once for only unresolved decisions. A common
question is:

> What are you moving, from where to where, and what volume or estimated value
> does the in-game inventory show?

Do not ask for location, ship, skills, owned hulls, visible contracts, or public
route activity when MCP can retrieve them. If the cargo can be found in assets,
confirm only the live facts whose cache age could change the plan.

## Specific courier contracts

For a supplied or visible courier contract, check:

- pickup and loaded routes, including security bands and current coverage;
- package volume against the intended hold and number of trips—the package
  itself cannot be split;
- reward, collateral, wallet affordability, expiry, and completion time;
- destination structure identity and access or delivery uncertainty;
- suspicious reward/collateral relationships or a route that depends on
  unconfirmed private access;
- whether the reward remains sensible after pickup travel, loaded travel, fuel
  or tolls, support, and risk.

Give `accept`, `decline`, or `needs one verification` only as advice. Do not
open the Plastic Wrap, promise structure access, or accept the contract. Avoid
false precision in loss probability or universal ISK-per-jump rules.

## Preparation output

Return a compact result:

1. **Move:** cargo, origin, destination, value/collateral, and assumptions.
2. **Primary ship:** owned or affordable hull, trip count, and why it fits the
   job.
3. **Fit proof:** EFT-style fit when requested or material, analyzer result,
   and unsupported hauling metrics.
4. **Route:** pickup leg when needed, primary loaded route, and one useful
   fallback only when justified.
5. **Procedure:** load split, travel method, arrival check, delivery, and abort
   triggers.
6. **Unknowns:** only facts that could change the recommendation.

Keep ordinary results short. Do not add a full market analysis, training plan,
fleet composition, or advanced-space appendix unless requested.
