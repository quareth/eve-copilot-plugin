# Site safety and eligibility

Classify the exact resolved site name before recommending entry. Site names and
hazards change; use the connected private EVE guide first, then current official
or maintained community material when the exact site is not known.

## Decision classes

### Recognized ordinary pirate data or relic site

Recommend entry only when the exact site is recognized as unguarded and the
active fit can scan and hack it. Continue normal Local/D-scan awareness. The
presence of hackable cans alone does not prove the site is ordinary.

### Sleeper-guarded wormhole site

Treat wormhole data/relic names containing **Unsecured** or **Forgotten** as
guarded Sleeper content unless current reliable evidence proves otherwise.
Do not send a pure exploration frigate in. Require a fit-specific combat and
tank assessment, or scout then commit a suitable combat ship.

### Ghost site or covert research facility

Treat as timed and hazardous. Failed hacks, container explosions, and NPC
arrival can punish an unsuitable fit. Confirm the exact variant, current
mechanics, tank, hacking plan, and exit before recommending entry. Do not learn
the timer by sacrificing an expensive ship.

### Sleeper Cache

Limited, Standard, and Superior Sleeper Cache sites contain different
environmental and room hazards. Resolve the exact site and room procedure from
current guidance. Require appropriate scan strength, hacking ability, tank,
mobility, and escape knowledge.

### Unknown, partial, or conflicting name

Answer "hold/skip while I verify" and look up the exact string. If reliable
classification remains unavailable, recommend skipping it with the current
ship. Never turn uncertainty into permission to enter.

## Eligibility proof

Before saying "run it," establish:

1. exact site identity and security-space context;
2. whether NPCs, timers, explosions, gas, clouds, sentries, or room mechanics
   are expected;
3. whether the active ship's scan, analyzer, tank, combat, and escape
   capabilities meet those hazards;
4. whether live Local/D-scan or wormhole-chain evidence makes entry sensible;
5. an abort trigger and an exit that the pilot can execute.

Use "recognized and fit-compatible" rather than "safe." Other pilots can still
interrupt an otherwise ordinary site.
