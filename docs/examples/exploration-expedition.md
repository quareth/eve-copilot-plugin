This is a real EVE Copilot session transcript, lightly formatted for
readability. ChatGPT interface timestamps and response-duration labels were
removed. Market prices, activity, routes, and character state are snapshots
from that session.

### Why Kheram

Kheram is confirmed 0.4 low-sec. The completed one-hour ESI snapshot showed:

- Kheram: 16 jumps, no ship/pod-kill row
- Unefsih: 11 jumps, no ship/pod-kill row
- Valmu: 12 jumps, 0 ship/pod kills
- Sibot: 10 jumps, 1 ship kill — exclude it initially

This suggests an under-contested candidate pocket, not a guarantee of sites or
safety.

### Procedure in every system

1. Hold gate cloak and inspect Local.
2. D-scan 360°, maximum range. Any combat probes, interdictor, recon, or
   suspicious ship means leave.
3. Warp cloaked away, make a safe spot, launch probes, then cloak.
4. Scan data and relic signatures.
5. Enter only exact, recognized ordinary pirate hacking sites.
6. Skip anything containing AEGIS, SCC, Unsecured, Forgotten, Covert,
   Operation Center, or Sleeper Cache.
7. While hacking, stay aligned and refresh D-scan every few seconds.

Extract immediately if Local spikes, combat probes appear, an unknown ship
lands, you accumulate roughly 50 million ISK, or fewer than 20 minutes remain.
Move on after five empty systems or about fifteen minutes without runnable
content.

Fallback: Gyerzen had only 5 jumps and no ship/pod kills in the same snapshot,
but it is a dead end with one exit through Dabrid, so use it only if the Kheram
branch is occupied.

When you enter Kheram, tell me the Local count and what appears on D-scan; I’ll
direct the next move.
